# Writing Rules

Applies to the plan, checklist, and diagnosis this skill returns.

- One line per point, at most 15 words, unless the document's format sets another limit.
- Fragments. Drop articles, filler, hedging, restated context: "Export button shows when flag off. Hide it." not "The Export button is currently displayed even though the preference is disabled, so it should be hidden."
- Code, identifiers, paths, error strings stay exact.
- Present state only: no history, dates, other findings, or decision trail.
- Detail the next reader needs to act goes in the point; reference detail goes in `<sub>`.
