# Writing Rules

Applies to the plan, checklist, and diagnosis.

- One line per point, at most 15 words, unless the format sets another limit.
- Fragments: no articles, filler, hedging, restated context. "Export button shows when flag off. Hide it."
- Code, identifiers, paths, error strings stay exact.
- Present state only: no history, dates, other findings, decision trail.
- Detail the next reader needs goes in the point; reference detail in `<sub>`.
