# go-research

Research a ticket, requirements link, or tracker queue. In `justaway`, one fast worker reads the live source and maps relevant paths before planning. Call `go-research` directly for a brief on its own.

## What you get

- Requirements and acceptance criteria from the live source, with comments and proposals distinguished from agreed requirements.
- Related tickets and attachments, open questions, and relevant repository files and folders.
- Source links and any access or evidence gaps.

The brief goes to `state/triage.md` when saved. Without a resolved task directory or outside manager mode, choose whether to save or print it. Routine local reads stay with the thread doing the work.
